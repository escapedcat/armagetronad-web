WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW. It shows that every route out of this machine,
including all three game servers, goes through utun5, and that only loopback does
not -- which is why the dockerized container is the one opponent every gate in this
milestone ever beat. It does NOT show the exit policy itself: the Nym policy was
never read, and no packet was sent to a cooperating endpoint inside 4533-4599 to
demonstrate the block, because every endpoint in that range is somebody's game
server and probing one is what task 6 was told not to do. The confirming test is
the same gate from a network with no tunnel on the default route.
