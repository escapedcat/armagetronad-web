  ms      = the frame interval's p50 in that window, MAX_FPS 1000, CPU throttle 6x
  pre     = ms_to_first_draw_p50   (the yield, input, the simulation, render setup)
  ren     = ms_first_draw_to_swap_p50 (render submission)
  draws   = draws_per_frame, the mean over the window
  free    = seconds 8-17 of the grind arm's round (driving in the open)
  rim     = seconds 20-59 of the same round (held against the wall)
  a median or a maximum over a window is over that window's PER-SECOND series,
  so "max 20.3 ms" is the worst SECOND's p50, not the worst single frame
  (the worst single frames are raw_ms_max in table.txt)
  spike round = peak draws/frame over whole seconds 10-58 >= 200, Task 2's definition
  flat round  = the same peak below 200

  MEDIANS ARE EXACT AND PRINTED WITH THE DECIMALS THEY NEED. An even-length
  window (the rim window is 40 seconds) has no middle observation, so its
  median is the midpoint of two and keeps the extra decimal instead of being
  rounded away: the grind arm's rim median is 17.15 ms, not 17.1. Figures
  pooled over rounds are medians of the UNROUNDED per-round medians, not of
  rounded ones. rim-free is computed from the unrounded medians too.
